About the tournament

MORE THAN A GAME.

The Ultimate Football Cup brings together college teams, amateur players and football fans for a celebration of competition, teamwork and sporting spirit.

Our goal is to create a platform for emerging talent, encourage an active lifestyle and deliver an exciting experience for players and spectators alike.
